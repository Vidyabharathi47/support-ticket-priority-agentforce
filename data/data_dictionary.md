Data Dictionary

Overview

This document describes the fields used in the sample support ticket dataset for the Support Ticket Intelligence project.

Field Name| Description| Example
Ticket_ID| Unique identifier for each ticket| TKT-0001
Subject| Short title of the customer issue| Login Issue
Description| Details of the reported problem| Unable to log in
Priority| Urgency level of the ticket| High
Status| Current state of the ticket| New

Priority Levels

- High: Urgent issues that require prompt attention.
- Medium: Issues that require attention but are less urgent.
- Low: General enquiries or less urgent requests.

Ticket Status

- New: A newly created ticket.
- Open: A ticket that is being handled or awaiting resolution.

Notes

- The dataset contains sample data for demonstration purposes.
- Field names may be adjusted to match the actual Salesforce custom object.
- No real customer information should be included.
